# User Guide

This guide explains how an agent uses the Travel Agent Onboarding Hub.

## Logging In

Open the portal:

```text
http://127.0.0.1:5173
```

Enter your email address and password.

For demo testing, all demo accounts use:

```text
Password123!
```

Example agent login:

```text
david.smith@example.com
```

## Agent Dashboard

The dashboard is the agent's home page.

It shows the main onboarding areas, progress, and important next actions.

Agents use the sidebar to move between pages.

## My Profile

The profile stores personal and business details, including:

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Business name
- Address
- Postcode
- Commission bank details
- Current onboarding status

Agents can update their own profile details. Admin staff control important status changes.

## Membership And Payments

This page shows:

- Membership type
- Membership status
- Payment status
- Setup fee
- Monthly fee
- Last payment date
- Next payment date
- Payment history
- Recurring payment reference, when Stripe is connected
- Stripe invoice links, when Stripe is connected

When Stripe is connected, the portal can show the recurring payment reference and paid or failed invoices with invoice links and PDF links.

Agents can view membership and payment records, but they cannot create or edit payment records. Payment records are controlled by admin and can be updated from Stripe.

## Onboarding Checklist

The onboarding checklist shows everything an agent must complete before trading.

Typical steps include:

- Create account
- Complete profile
- Upload ID document
- Upload proof of address
- Add bank details
- Sign contractor agreement
- Accept membership terms
- Pay setup fee
- Set up recurring payment
- Attend welcome call
- Attend compliance call
- Complete required training
- Accept advertising and social media policy
- Complete final assessment
- Wait for admin final approval

Each step has a status, such as:

- Not Started
- In Progress
- Awaiting Review
- Complete
- Rejected
- Overdue

Some steps need admin approval before they count as complete.

For steps that need evidence, upload the contract or document in Documents & Agreements first. Then use the document link as the evidence link on the checklist step.

## Training Academy

The Training Academy contains required onboarding lessons.

Training may include:

- Text lessons
- Embedded videos
- Embedded PDF documents
- Quizzes
- Assessments
- Certificates

Mandatory training must be completed before final approval.

## Training Module Detail

Open a training module to see its content and progress.

A module may include:

- Description
- Written lesson content
- Embedded video content
- Embedded PDF content
- Quiz requirement
- Pass mark
- Certificate information

## Further Training

Further Training is for ongoing development after onboarding.

It may include:

- Sales Development
- Destination Knowledge
- Supplier Training
- Cruise Training
- Disney Training
- Luxury Travel
- Group Bookings
- Marketing
- Compliance Refreshers
- Leadership Training
- Systems Refreshers

Further Training stays locked until mandatory onboarding training is complete.

## Live Training And Calls

This page shows live sessions and attendance records.

Examples include:

- Welcome Call
- Compliance Call
- Systems Training Call
- Sales Process Call
- Marketing Call
- Supplier Training
- Final Sign-Off Call
- Refresher Training
- Team Meeting

Attendance becomes part of the agent's compliance record.

## Documents And Agreements

This page stores important records, such as:

- Contractor Agreement
- Membership Terms
- ID Document
- Proof of Address
- Bank Details Confirmation
- Social Media Policy
- GDPR Policy
- Compliance Policy
- Training Certificate

Admins can verify or reject documents.

This version can upload files into the local portal storage and can also record an existing document link. For a live online launch, uploaded files should be moved to proper cloud file storage.

## Compliance Centre

The Compliance Centre shows:

- Policies to accept
- Compliance training
- Compliance checklist
- Customer money handling rules
- Advertising and social media rules
- Complaints process
- Certificate and expiry information

Policy acceptance is recorded as proof that the agent has accepted important rules.

## Certificates

Certificates prove that training or assessments have been completed.

Certificate statuses include:

- Active
- Expired
- Revoked

Some certificates may need renewing later.

## Supplier Access

Supplier Access contains protected supplier links and instructions.

This area is locked until the agent is Approved to Trade.

## Marketing Hub

The Marketing Hub contains approved business resources, such as:

- Brand guidelines
- Logo files
- Social media templates
- Approved offer wording
- Advertising policy
- Pricing guidance
- Campaign assets
- Downloadable resources

This area is locked until the agent accepts the social media and advertising policy.

## Notifications

Notifications are messages inside the portal.

They may include:

- Payment reminders
- Failed payment alerts
- Training assigned
- Training overdue
- Call reminders
- Missed call alerts
- Document required
- Document rejected
- Approved to trade
- Certificate expiring

Email sending can be added later.

## Agent Statuses

An agent may move through statuses such as:

- Invited
- Registered
- Payment Pending
- Payment Active
- Onboarding In Progress
- Training In Progress
- Awaiting Final Approval
- Approved to Trade
- Active Agent
- Suspended
- Payment Overdue
- Compliance Hold
- Terminated
- Archived

The status helps staff understand what stage the agent is at.
